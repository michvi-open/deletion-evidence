Deletion Evidence Record — Reference App

v0.1 · runs entirely in your browser. Records are not transmitted or stored by Michvi.

This project is an open-source public contribution and is separate from Michvi LLP's confidential advisory engagements and internal methodologies. It records evidence of an asserted/executed deletion action — it is not independent proof that data was physically deleted, and it is not legal advice or a compliance determination.
