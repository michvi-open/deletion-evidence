# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
v2.1. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- accept feedback gracefully and give it the same way;
- assume good faith, especially in a project whose entire purpose is
  precise, careful disagreement about how evidence should be recorded.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language or jokes, and deliberately derailing technical discussion.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions
— and in any other setting when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent privately to
`contact@michvi.com`.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take
appropriate, proportionate corrective action in response to any behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
